We combine two importer tasks in one place: find and buy goods in China and bring them to Russia legally, with a full package of documents. Here is how it works, step by step.

## 1. Request and quote

You describe what you need to buy or ship. We clarify requirements, volume and budget, then prepare a quote and a commercial offer.

## 2. Contract with a Russian legal entity

We sign a contract with a company in Russia. Payment to a settlement account in rubles — no grey payments or chains of agents.

## 3. Sourcing and QC in China

Our team in Guangzhou works with factories directly: finds the manufacturer, audits the plant, brings samples, controls quality at production and buys the goods.

## 4. Documents and customs

We prepare the full set of documents, apply labelling to Russian requirements and clear customs with a declaration. This is **white delivery** — without the risk of additional charges or claims.

## 5. Delivery to your warehouse

We ship by the chosen method — road, rail, sea or air — to a warehouse in any city in Russia and hand over the cargo with a complete set of documents.
